Your data, your browser

Privacy & data

AuthentiCite is a static website. There is no AuthentiCite server, no account and no database, so there is nothing for us to store — your references stay with you.

✓We do not store your referencesPasted text and extracted references are processed in your browser tab and are gone when you close it.
✓We do not store your documentsPDF and Word files are read locally on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
✓We do not collect your email or identityNo sign-up, no login. The email field in Options is optional and never reaches us.
✓No trackingNo analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies.

What happens when you convert & verify

To check that a reference exists, your browser sends small pieces of that reference — typically the title, first author, year, and a DOI, arXiv id or ISBN if present — directly from your device to public scholarly databases. The request does not pass through any AuthentiCite server.

ServiceUsed forWhat it receives
CrossrefDOI and article lookupReference text / title / DOI
OpenAlexArticle lookup, retraction statusTitle / DOI
Semantic ScholarArticle and arXiv lookupTitle / DOI / arXiv id
Open Library, Google BooksBook lookupTitle, author, ISBN
doi.orgChecking that a DOI existsThe DOI

These services see your IP address like any website does and have their own privacy policies. You can switch any of them off in Options on the Converter page. Nothing is sent to a large language model or any AI service.

Documents you upload

PDF, DOCX and text files are opened and read inside your browser. Only the text of the references (as above) is ever used for lookups. To read PDF and Word files the page downloads two open-source helper libraries (PDF.js and Mammoth) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com the first time you open such a file — that is a download to you; your file is not sent there.

Email address (optional)

The Your email box in Options is optional. If you fill it in, it is stored only in your own browser and is added to Crossref and OpenAlex requests as a courtesy mailto parameter, which those services use to contact heavy users and give them more reliable access. AuthentiCite does not receive or keep it. Leave it empty if you prefer.

What is saved in your browser

A few small items are kept in your browser's local storage on your device only, to make the tool convenient:

  • Theme — light or dark.
  • Settings — your Options choices (and the optional email, if you typed one).
  • Draft — the text in the input box, so a refresh doesn't lose it.
  • Usage counters — how many references you have checked, shown on the About page.

Other things worth knowing

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub, like any web host, may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address) for the pages and files you load; AuthentiCite has no access to them.
  • Fonts. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them.
  • About page. It asks GitHub's public API for the repository's star and fork counts. No personal data is sent.
  • Suggestions page. It does not send anything by itself. When you press the button it opens a pre-filled GitHub issue in a new tab; nothing is posted until you submit it there, and GitHub issues are public — don't include private details.
  • Exports. The .bib and .csv files are created on your device and saved by your browser. We never see them.

Questions or concerns

The whole site is open source — you can read exactly what the code does at github.com/Hassanahmed1166/ref-to-bibtex. If something here is unclear or you spot a privacy problem, please tell us.
